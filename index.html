Service period — used by all three below

Leave and severance count every month between these dates — they do not depend on which hours were captured on the time sheet. Only the grant's percentage uses what this sheet recorded.

Botswana's minimum is 15 working days a year — 1.25 a month, counted over the service period above.

Botswana's severance benefit is one day's basic pay for each month of the first 60, and two days a month after that — counted over the service period above.
